## What the deck corrected

- **History was wrong.** I had Elements 2024 as the origin. The deck cites
  Elements '23, '24, '25, Love Burn '24, '25, Showcation '25 — activations
  since 2023, and the crew itself goes back a decade before that. Elements
  2023 and Love Burn 2024 have no Drive folder, so they're now on the past
  page with a note.
- **Shelly was missing entirely.** Now on the landing page, the technical
  page, and in the role structure.
- **Roles were vague.** "Section Leads" replaced with the actual structure:
  Camp Lead, four lead positions (Production BOH, Production FOH, Art Car,
  Artist Relations), two officer positions (Financial, Logistics), and the
  12-core-member floor.
- **The activation description was thin.** Day lounge vs. night dancefloor,
  tile flooring, exterior stacks, tree uplighting, fire pits, the DJ split
  between residents and drop-in festival artists.
- **Footprint and build timeline** added — 60x60 min, 120x60 ideal, one day
  build, half day strike.

   (@the_jellyfish_collective) and names Nic as point of contact for
   Showcation. Instagram DMs are now the default on the drafts. If you want a
   real front door, an email alias is better — Discord invites are too open.

   quantities, and it goes to festival organizers, so it's less private than I
   assumed. The technical page currently names manufacturers but not the
   quantity table. You could publish the full table as a service to other
   camps, or keep counts internal. Your call.

3. **Dues on the public site.** Showcation '26 was $250, Elements '26 was
   $275. Currently no figure is published. A range ("dues typically run
   $250–300 per activation") might be the honest middle.

4. **Showcation 2026 entry.** Written from the pitch deck, which describes
   what was *proposed*. Confirm what actually got built before publishing.

5. **Love Burn spelling.** The deck uses "Love Burn," your Drive folders use
   "Loveburn." Drafts use the deck's version.

6. **The Izakaya.** It's a lead assignment and a budget line for Showcation
   '26, currently one sentence on the past page. If it's a recurring thing it
