A jellyfish has no brain, no heart, and no spine. It has outlived five mass
extinctions anyway. We took notes.

No president, no board, no founder collecting rent on an idea. Here is the
whole machine.

---

## Who's in charge

Nobody, permanently.

For each activation somebody volunteers as Camp Lead. They own the vision,
the budget, and the shape of that one event. When the last stake comes out of
the ground, that authority evaporates and the next lead starts from zero.

Under the lead, section leads run Infrastructure, Kitchen, Sound and
Lighting, and Decor, each with a team of jellies. Officers handle finance,
logistics, music booking, and more. Anyone who would rather build one
specific thing can take a project instead: an art car, a structure, or an
activation nobody asked for.

Show up with an idea or energy to give and you are already one of us.

---

## How it's paid for

By us. Every time.

Each activation collects camp dues, split evenly among everyone camping,
covering infrastructure, decor, production, food, fuel, and logistics. There
is no group bank account, no owner, and no profit. Nobody has ever been paid
for an hour of work here, including the leads.

Every purchase gets logged in a shared spreadsheet down to the dollar,
visible to every member and kept on record permanently. When festivals award
us grant tickets, they get sold back to members at cost and that money
lowers everyone's dues. Nobody rides free.

---

## Jelly Credits

In every volunteer group on earth, a handful of people quietly carry an
enormous share of the weight and then burn out because nobody saw it.

So we count it. Build day, strike day, shifts worked, truck driving, leading
a team, and troubleshooting the problems no one else could figure out. At the
end of the cycle a pool of camp money gets divided across the credits earned,
and a real chunk of your dues comes back based on what you actually did.

Nobody is fined and nobody is shamed. It just means the person who drove the
truck through the night does not have to wonder whether anyone noticed.

---

## Working with us

If you are thinking about camping with us, fair warning: this is a
participation camp, not a hotel. Everyone builds, everyone takes a shift,
everyone helps tear down. That is the whole deal, and it is why it works.
