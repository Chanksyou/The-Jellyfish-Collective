Hanging jellyfish light installation, upgraded audio, and custom tile flooring
for an after-hours dancefloor built to keep people comfortable until sunrise.
Outside: additional speaker and sub stacks, exterior lasers, tree uplighting,
and bench seating with fire pits. Shelly ran synced to the main system.

By day the same footprint ran as an open-field lounge — hammocks, carpets, and
open-sky lounging, with resident and guest DJs trading off against spontaneous
sets from Showcation artists.

Also home to the Izakaya, a side project that ran alongside the main activation.
