## Before every activation

Every member reviews and signs off on three things. It isn't a formality, and
"I skimmed it" isn't sign-off.

1. **[Camp expectations and responsibilities](/how-it-works/)** — what you're
   committing to: meetings, build, strike, shifts, dues.
2. **[Safety guidelines](/technical/)** —
   generators, fuel, cable, load management. Read this even if you never
   expect to touch the electrical.
3. **Consent** — below.

---

## Consent

The short version, which is also the whole version: **ask first, every time.**

- Consent is asked for and given, not assumed from silence, from what someone
  is wearing, from where they are, or from what happened last year.
- It's specific. Yes to one thing is not yes to another.
- It's revocable at any point, including partway through, including without a
  reason.
- Someone who is drunk, high, or asleep can't give it.
- **This includes photography.** Ask before you shoot, and ask again before
  you post. Our activations run late and people are relaxed — that isn't
  permission to put someone on the internet.

If you're unsure whether you have consent, you don't. Ask.

New members: watch the "Tea and Consent" video before your first activation.
It's two minutes and it's the clearest explanation anyone has made.

**If something happens** — to you or to someone else — go to a Camp Lead or
any Safety Guardian. If you'd rather not go to someone in camp, every event we
attend has its own safety team, and they're there for exactly this. Raising it
is never an overreaction, and it will not be treated as drama.

## Where things live

**Planning docs and rosters** — your Camp Lead shares these with the roster
for that activation. They aren't published here, because they have people's
phone numbers and addresses in them.

**Discord** — meeting minutes, day-to-day coordination, the place decisions
actually get made between meetings. Ask a Camp Lead for an invite.

**This site** — the durable stuff. If something is true every year, it belongs
on these pages. If it's true only this year, it belongs in the planning docs.

## Roles for the current activation

Who leads what changes per activation and gets posted physically in camp.
Broadly:

- **Camp Lead** — runs the activation end to end
- **Production BOH** — sound, light, stage, DJs
- **Production FOH** — decor, the vibe, communal infrastructure
- **Art Car** — Shelly
- **Artist Relations** — residents, guests, drop-in sets
- **Logistics** — transport, storage, load-in and load-out
- **Financial** — dues, budget, reimbursements
