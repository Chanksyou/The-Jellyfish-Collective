## No application, but not automatic

There's no paperwork, no membership fee, and no waiting list. But joining
isn't automatic either: every new member needs to be vouched for by a few
current jellies, or sit down with us for a short conversation, so we can
make sure you understand what you're signing up for and that it's a good fit
on both sides.

Most people arrive because they know someone, or because they wandered into
the lounge at 3am and came back the next day to ask how it got there. From
there, a vouch or a conversation is what turns showing up into joining.

## What you're signing up for

- Camp meetings and planning sessions in the months before an event.
  Attendance is mandatory and participation is the point.
- A full day of build and half a day of strike, including leave no trace.
- A shift during the event, assigned by whoever leads your area.
- Camp dues for that activation, split equally.

If money is the barrier, say so early. If time is the barrier, say so early.
Both have been worked around before — with alternative contributions, expert
services, equipment loans, or adjusted dues. What doesn't work is going quiet
and hoping it resolves itself.
